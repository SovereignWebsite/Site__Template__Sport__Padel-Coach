Designs, by design.c

Five designs for one padel coach

The same words, the same week and the same court lab, in five directions. Each one works on a phone, in English and Portuguese, light and dark. Pick one to open it; the live page is at the home page.

  • 1. Matchday Dia de jogo

    A broadcast scoreboard: fixtures, a score bug and a replay panel.

  • 2. Algarve Light Luz do Algarve

    Photograph-led and editorial, from morning light to evening.

  • 3. Court Lines Linhas do court

    The court is the grid: every section framed by its lines.

  • 4. Club Poster Cartaz do clube

    A bold club poster with tile borders, in two languages at once.

  • 5. Night Session Sessão noturna

    The floodlit evening court, dark by default.
